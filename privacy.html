Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Policy explains how Profitcentral SLU (Spain) processes information in the Reform AI iOS app. Contact: contact@profitcentral.net.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data. The App creates a Supabase account with a random identifier. You may optionally link Sign in with Apple; Apple may provide a stable identifier and, if you choose, a relay or real email. We never receive your Apple ID password.
  • Content. Room photos, AI-generated images, selected room/style and optional prompts.
  • Subscription data. Product, transaction identifiers, trial/renewal state, expiration and refunds received from Apple. Apple processes payment details.
  • Product interaction and device data. Paywall, checkout, trial and purchase events, app/device information, and advertising identifiers only when iOS permission allows. Firebase/Google and Meta receive conversion events for our advertising. Apple provides privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork attribution. Photos, prompts and generated images are never sent to these providers.
  • Diagnostics. Apple may provide aggregated crash diagnostics if you enable sharing in iOS.

PostHog analytics is currently disabled. This Policy will be updated before it is enabled.

2. Why we use it

We use data to provide authentication, AI generation and cross-device history; verify subscriptions; prevent abuse; provide support; understand reliability and conversion; and measure our advertising. We do not sell personal data, show third-party ads inside the App, or use photos to train AI models.

4. Tracking choices

SKAdNetwork works without identifying you across apps. Apple's advertising identifier is available only after App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, Meta and Google do not receive it. Change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, portability, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. Email contact@profitcentral.net. EU users may complain to their authority; in Spain, the AEPD.

7. Children and security

The App is not directed to children under 13. We use HTTPS, private Storage with Row-Level Security, short-lived signed URLs, Supabase authentication and server-only privileged credentials. No system is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy and will change the date above. Material changes will be communicated as required.
